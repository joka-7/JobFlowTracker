Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

JobFlow Tracker tracks a job search, a hiring pipeline or tasks, in your browser or in the Android app. It has no accounts of its own and no server or database that holds your data, so the person who runs JobFlow Tracker cannot read it.

What stays on your device

Your companies, candidates, tasks, notes and settings are saved in this browser's (or the app's) local storage. Nothing leaves the device unless you turn on one of the two optional features below.

Optional: sync with your Google Drive

If you choose Connect Drive, you sign in with Google and JobFlow Tracker saves your data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. It asks only for access to that folder (the drive.appdata permission) plus your name and email so it can show who is connected. It cannot see, list or change any other file in your Drive, and the hidden folder is not visible to anyone but you and this app. Your sign-in token and email are kept on your device.

JobFlow Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Drive is used only to sync your data between your devices.

Optional: AI features

If you add an AI provider and your own API key, the text you send to it (for example a job description, company names or what you type in the chat) goes straight from your device to the AI provider you choose, under your account with them. It does not pass through anything run by JobFlow Tracker, and that provider's own privacy terms apply. Your API key is stored on your device only. Avoid typing other people's personal details into AI features.

The website

This site is a set of static files on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may keep standard request logs (such as your IP address). JobFlow Tracker itself does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Removing your data

  • In Settings, choose Delete synced data & disconnect. It deletes JobFlow Tracker's files from your Drive, revokes the app's access, and removes this device's copy. Other devices that are still connected will upload their copies again, so disconnect them first.
  • You can also remove JobFlow Tracker's access and its hidden data yourself in your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services.
  • Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the Android app, removes the copy on that device.
